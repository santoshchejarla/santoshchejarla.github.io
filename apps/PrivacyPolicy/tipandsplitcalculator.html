Privacy Policy for Split & Tip Calculator

Last updated: 05/08/2025 - Aug 5th 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Split & Tip Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

About Our App

Split & Tip Calculator is a simple utility application that helps users calculate tips and split bills among groups. The app performs mathematical calculations locally on your device and does not require an internet connection to function.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Bill Amount: When you enter a bill amount, this information is processed locally on your device for calculation purposes only.
  • Tip Percentage: Your selected tip percentage (0%, 15%, 18%, 20%, or custom) is used for calculations.
  • Group Size: The number of people splitting the bill is used for calculation purposes.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Any data that could identify you personally

How We Use Your Information

All calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not:

  • Store your input data on our servers
  • Share your information with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Track your usage patterns
  • Collect any personal information

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All calculations and user inputs are processed locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No persistent storage of your calculation history

Security Measures

  • The app operates entirely offline
  • No network permissions are required
  • No data is stored in cloud services

Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The app functions completely independently without external dependencies.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, this app is safe for users of all ages.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. All calculations are performed locally and are not retained.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
